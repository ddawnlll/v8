Document status and reading rule

PRE-EXPERIMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BOUND. This document does not claim that V8 is profitable, trader intuition is reliable, or execution has been validated. Its purpose is to separate sourced ideas, design assumptions, and propositions that must be eliminated or supported by experiments.

Labels have the following meanings: LITERATURE_SUPPORTED is a bounded externally supported claim; PROJECT_EVIDENCE_SUPPORTED is a local project finding; DESIGN_INFERENCE is a testable engineering inference; PROVISIONAL_DECISION is reversible; LOCKED_INVARIANT is a validity rule; OPEN_QUESTION is unresolved; and REJECTED_OPTION is excluded from the initial baseline.

Abstract

V8 compares the universal-model premise—produce one directional prediction for every market timestamp—with a falsifiable alternative: decompose conditional trading reasoning into explicit, behavior-specific decision modules. The central question is whether this decomposition can produce more useful and robust decisions than an equal-information global timestamp-level predictor.

That question is not one hypothesis. The existence of behavioral structure, concentration of edge in specific habitats, value of Expert decomposition, value of a Candidate Episode data model, value of selection/quality filtering, need for ranking under capital scarcity, and interaction of execution with alpha must be tested separately. Literature supports the relevance of liquidity, order flow, volatility clustering, market impact, selective prediction, and multiple-testing risk. It does not establish that V8 Experts, a Router, or a Scorer have economic superiority.

Accordingly, the initial architecture is deliberately small: point-in-time MarketState → 2–3 deterministic self-gating Experts → append-only record of the complete Candidate lifecycle → deterministic acceptance/risk → one canonical Level-1 execution ledger. Router, learned Scorer, Ranker, RL execution, and order-book simulation are not defaults. Each must show value against a simpler deterministic baseline in a preregistered, costed, frozen out-of-sample experiment.

Problem definition

The wrongly posed problem: predict direction at every bar

A global model compresses heterogeneous processes—trend, range, volatility shock, liquidity depletion, forced liquidation, continuation, and reversal—into one target function. Strong in-sample classification does not show that a signal is selectable after costs, tied to a mechanism, or robust to regime change. The file-grounded V7 evidence makes this distinction necessary: one specific bar-derived feature/executor campaign did not clear its stated economic gate under its stated cost assumptions. That result neither validates nor refutes all behavioral edge or V8 itself.

The V8 research problem

V8 decomposes “is the next price move up or down?” into four questions: (1) Is there an observable behavior that could be economically meaningful at this time? (2) Can it be formalized ex ante with habitat, setup, trigger, invalidation, and expiry? (3) Once recorded as a Candidate, which hypotheses trigger and what happens under a canonical cost and execution policy? (4) Does a more complex component add measured value beyond the simpler baseline?

Success and failure criteria

Success is not an attractive backtest or complex diagram. A component is admitted only when it clears a preregistered target with complete trial lineage, point-in-time data, fixed cost/fill assumptions, dependence-aware uncertainty, multiple-testing accounting, and untouched chronological OOS. Failure is also information: reject an Expert, omit a Router, or simplify a Candidate schema. V8’s output is therefore a research laboratory that ties architectural complexity to burden of evidence, not a signal machine.

Introduction: why this research is necessary

The most expensive error in trading research is to mistake an observed price shape for a cause, and a selected backtest for edge. A second error is to conceal the first with more features, a larger model, finer routing, or more realistic-looking execution. This monograph keeps economic mechanism, observable behavior, statistical signature, trade rule, and execution choice distinct. Positive evidence at one layer never automatically settles the next layer.

The document does two jobs. First, it deeply analyzes the accessible work in the supplied 60-entry list across MoE/routing, selective prediction, survival and multi-state lifecycle methods, temporal event modeling, overfitting controls, microstructure, impact, and execution/portfolio decisions. Second, it draws the boundary on transfer: LLM MoE evidence is not financial Expert value; medical survival models are not Candidate PnL; contemporaneous order-flow impact is not forecastability. The valid transfer is primarily at the level of test design and data contracts.
