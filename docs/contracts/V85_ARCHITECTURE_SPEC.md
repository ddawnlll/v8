# Abstract

V8.5 changes the question from *“which historical benchmark looks best?”* to
*“what evidence is sufficient to justify using this frozen swing policy to compound capital under the intended deployment conditions?”*
The economic objective is long-horizon, after-cost geometric capital growth; calendar return targets are reporting goals and must never
force trades, shorten holding periods, relax admission, or induce noise-chasing.

The architecture remains evidence-first. Real historical replay, frozen OOS, synthetic and adversarial worlds, Expert qualification,
the three Oracles, statistical controls, agent/Kaizen TEVV, shadow observations and eventually live receipts are different evidence
sources with different authority. They are not averaged into a readiness score. Instead, V8.5 introduces an **Assurance Fabric**:
a typed connection layer that binds provenance, authority, claims, admissibility rules, defeaters and deployment verdicts.
The design borrows the structure—not the domain conclusions—of formal assurance cases, provenance standards, model-risk validation,
stress testing and AI TEVV. [R02][R03][R06][R07][R08]

Synthetic data is promoted to a first-class falsification substrate but remains forbidden from proving economic edge.
Generator realism is a vector, not a scalar, and learned generators are challengers rather than the V8.5 baseline.
System-level reverse stress testing searches for plausible failure surfaces rather than merely replaying named crises.
Agentic Kaizen is itself evaluated for contamination and grader gaming. [R04][R05][R09][R10][R11]

The Federal Reserve's 2026 model-risk guidance separates conceptual soundness, outcome analysis and ongoing monitoring,
and stresses independent effective challenge and model limitations. This is used as an architectural analogy for V8.5's
specification/verification, empirical evaluation and continuous monitoring lanes. [R02]
NIST's 2026 TEVV-Athlon draft similarly treats TEVV as a customizable assessment process tied to a use case rather than a
single universal benchmark. [R03]

# Production Growth Contract

## 1. Objective

$$
maximize   LGNG = Long-Horizon After-Cost Geometric Capital Growth

LGNG is measured over an evaluation horizon long enough to contain complete swing campaigns and is reported with
weekly-equivalent, monthly-equivalent and annualized views. Equivalent-period figures are reporting transforms; they are not
calendar trading quotas.

## 2. Project performance ambition

**Project target:** approximately 2–3% weekly-equivalent geometric after-cost growth is the current production ambition;
approximately 10% weekly-equivalent is a stretch “Kızıl Elma” objective. These are *targets, not claims*, and are not encoded
as instructions to trade more frequently. They must be evaluated against drawdown, tail, friction, execution and forward-evidence constraints.

**Ratification boundary:** these numeric ambitions are non-authoritative `PlanningAmbition` metadata. They are
not inputs to policy selection, challenger ranking, compute allocation, stopping, admission, sizing, exits, promotion or readiness. A change
to the ambition must leave policy, case, trial, trade, verdict and certificate hashes bit-identical; only an explicitly non-authoritative
planning view may change.

## 3. Contract schema

```text
ProductionGrowthContract {
    contract_id
    policy_lineage_id

    initial_capital
    objective = LONG_HORIZON_GEOMETRIC_NET_GROWTH

At M0, the contract must define deterministic treatment of initial/current equity, realized and unrealized PnL,
fees, funding, slippage, open positions, drawdown and geometric growth. Missing values are explicit
`INCOMPLETE_ECONOMICS`/`UNKNOWN`; they are never replaced by zero. FX, multi-collateral and capacity
extensions remain later contracts and cannot silently elevate authority.

# Deployment-equivalent evaluation and universe semantics

V8.5 distinguishes **opportunity universe breadth** from **capital allocation breadth**.
A ten-symbol universe means ten assets may produce opportunities; it does not require ten allocations.

```text
Opportunity Universe: BTC, ETH, SOL, AVAX, BNB, ...
                         │
                         ▼
                PIT Select / Abstain
                         │
              ┌──────────┼──────────┐
              ▼          ▼          ▼
             BTC        AVAX       others
             80%        20%         0%

```

A BTC-only deployment is valid if the selector/allocator reaches it using decision-time information and if the policy is frozen
before the evaluated future. It is invalid to inspect realized returns, choose BTC after the fact, then relabel the result as forward evidence.

| Experiment | Question | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deployment-equivalent | What would this exact production policy have done with actual capital, sizing, lot steps and costs? | Economic replication when protected. |
| Normalized alpha isolation | Did the signal itself degrade when universe changed, holding per-asset risk/capital constant? | Diagnostic / mechanism. |
| Universe expansion | Can the selector reject newly-added low-quality assets rather than trading them mechanically? | Selection / robustness. |
| Asset-only specialization | Is BTC-only or AVAX-only a viable explicitly preregistered policy class? | Economic only after protected evaluation. |

# Assurance Fabric

The Assurance Fabric is not an Oracle and not a competing evidence engine. It is the missing composition layer between
D-136 evidence production and Kaizen's normative verdict. The structure is influenced by formal assurance-case practice
and provenance models, adapted to V8's economic authority system. [R06][R07]

# Authority Projection (read-only canonical view)

Current V8 carries several useful but differently-granular authority vocabularies: the constitutional
Evidence × Decision × Realization tensor, D-136's epistemic evidence classes, and Oracle L1/L2/L3 plus identifiability.
V8.5 should preserve each distinction without implicit lossy conversion.

```text
AuthorityProjection {

        OBSERVED | MODEL_DERIVED | SYNTHETIC | COUNTERFACTUAL

    inference_method:
        DIRECT | DETERMINISTIC_DERIVATION | STATISTICAL_ESTIMATE |
        DETERMINISTIC_COUNTERFACTUAL | OFF_POLICY_ESTIMATE | ORACLE_BOUND

        L0_CONTRACT | L1_BAR_REPLAY | L2_EXOGENOUS_MICROSTRUCTURE |

        IDENTIFIED | PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED | MODEL_DERIVED | NOT_IDENTIFIABLE

    decision_authority:
        DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY | RECONCILED | UTILITY_ELIGIBLE |
        PORTFOLIO_AUTHORIZED | EXECUTION_AUTHORIZED

    realization:
        HYPOTHETICAL | SIMULATED | SHADOW_OBSERVED | FILLED | CASHFLOW_SETTLED

    value_notion:
        RETROSPECTIVE | REPLICATION | PROSPECTIVE | LIVE_REALIZED

    data_role_id
    assumptions[]
    dependency_hashes[]
}
```

**Design law:** normalization may add dimensions but must never silently upgrade an input.
Any mapping from an existing V8 authority type to `AuthorityProjection` must be total, testable and non-escalating.

This is a read-only canonical projection over the existing `crate::authority::Authority`; it cannot mint authority, write
the ClaimRegistry, reclassify a claim, issue a verdict or create a promotion path. Until a future ratified supersession, the existing
three-dimensional Authority remains the sole statutory authorization root. Safety is defined by
`AdmissibleClaims(output) ⊆ AdmissibleClaims(input)`; missing or incomparable dimensions yield `UNKNOWN`/`UNMAPPED`
and fail closed.

# Assurance claim taxonomy and readiness states

Assurance claims are deliberately separate from the existing six statutory economic claim classes.
They answer “is the burden of proof for this operational state met?” rather than “what economic value class is this number?”

| Assurance claim | Typical burden |
| --- | --- |
| ENGINEERING_INTEGRITY | Parity, PIT, deterministic replay, hash binding, ledger conservation, no forbidden path. |
| SEMANTIC_INTEGRITY | D-141 Expert passports and system contract invariants. |
| RESEARCH_INTEGRITY | Trial lineage, holdout access discipline, agent TEVV, no test tampering or omitted failures. |
| SYSTEM_ROBUSTNESS | Qualified world families, reverse stress, cost/liquidity/correlation perturbations, no critical ruin defeater. |
| ECONOMIC_REPLICATION | Protected real-tape after-cost evidence under deployment-equivalent contracts plus statistical plan. |
| PROSPECTIVE_VALIDITY | Frozen policy, future-only observations, shadow evidence and operational stability. |
| SHADOW_READY | ALL_OF engineering, semantic, research, robustness and minimum economic replication claims. |
| LIMITED_LIVE_READY | SHADOW_READY + prospective evidence + live risk/capacity/rollback contract. |
| LIVE_SUPPORTED | Observed live receipts under the intended policy version, with monitoring still active. |

```text
SHADOW_READY
    = ALL_OF(
        ENGINEERING_INTEGRITY,
        SEMANTIC_INTEGRITY,
        RESEARCH_INTEGRITY,
        SYSTEM_ROBUSTNESS,
        ECONOMIC_REPLICATION
      )

PIT_LEAK => ENGINEERING_INTEGRITY = FALSIFIED
          => SHADOW_READY = BLOCKED
```

# Market World Foundry

The Foundry generates finite, hashable market worlds for qualification, stress and falsification. For the M0 ratification candidate,
synthetic full-chain outputs are restricted to deterministic `#[cfg(test)]` sabotage/integration harnesses and carry no
production EvaluationCase, report, certificate or economic authority. A future ratified D-series may expand this boundary only with
transitive taint, negative-only synthetic authority and separate capability/storage controls. V8.5 does not treat
synthetic generation as a substitute for real economic evidence. Recent financial generation work reports that visually realistic
and stylized-fact-matching GANs can still produce unstable or implausible behavior in trading backtests; multivariate correlation
generation remains an explicit design challenge. [R10][R11]

# Reverse stress testing

Basel stress-testing principles explicitly call for reverse stress tests that begin with an adverse outcome and ask which
extreme-but-plausible conditions could produce it. Adaptive Stress Testing formalizes a related simulation search for likely
failure trajectories in sequential systems. V8.5 adopts the structure as a falsification tool, without importing probability claims
that the simulator cannot support. [R08][R09]

```text
Declared failure:
    MaxDD >= 20%
        or
    liquidation
        or
    FrictionRetention <= 0
        or
    PIT/authority invariant breach

ReverseStressSearch:
    find world parameters / trajectory
    minimizing plausibility-distance
    subject to failure condition
```

The primary output is a `ReverseStressReceipt` containing the failure trajectory, declared plausibility metric,
world-family version, search algorithm/version, seeds, and minimal counterexample where practical.

# Statistical Court and research multiplicity

Backtest selection is a research process, not a single test. White's Reality Check, DSR, PBO/CSCV and Harvey–Liu style
multiple-testing adjustments all address the fact that repeated strategy search can inflate apparent performance. V8.5 treats
these as tools selected by a declared StatisticalPlan, not as magic badges. [R12][R13][R14][R15][R16]

```text
StatisticalPlan {
    family_id
    primary_metric
    null / benchmark
    chronology
    clustering / block unit
    dependence treatment
    multiplicity family
    total trials known / estimated
    stopping rule
    confidence / equivalence criteria
    missing-data policy
    test algorithms + versions
}
```

## Locked rules

- AI-generated and automatically discarded candidates count toward research debt.

- Repeated access to the same holdout changes its role; it cannot remain pristine by naming convention.

- Chronological dependence is not repaired by random IID splitting.

- A statistical test whose assumptions are unavailable yields `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, not a placeholder p-value.

- Until a ratified supersession is registered, the active V8 requirement that WRC, genuine DSR and Hansen SPA receipts support
`SUPPORTED_EDGE` remains in force. A future assumption-aware `StatisticalPlan` may replace a method only through
an explicit D-series decision, pre-outcome `MethodSubstitutionReceipt` and equal-or-stronger error-control evidence.

- DSR confidence and p-values are distinct typed quantities; missing, underpowered, invalid or proxy DSR/PBO outputs are
`BLOCKING_IMPLEMENTATION_DEBT` and cannot carry economic authority.

- Economic magnitude and after-cost utility remain necessary even when statistical evidence is favorable.

# Shadow, limited live and continuous monitoring

Prospective shadow is a separate authority stage: a frozen policy observes only information arriving after its cutoff.
The baseline V8 shadow design already seals code/config/dataset/authority identities and forbids self-promotion.
V8.5 preserves that boundary and routes ShadowReceipt back into the Assurance Fabric. Model-risk guidance's emphasis on
ongoing monitoring supports the lifecycle principle that validation is not finished at first approval. [R02][R17]

## Continuous monitoring defeaters

- Code/config drift from certified artifact.

- Data/venue schema drift invalidating assumptions.

- Execution cost or slippage outside certified sensitivity band.

- Persistent Target DTE collapse or new selection pathology.

- Tail/risk threshold breach.

- Research-integrity or provenance break.

# Why this is V8.5, not V9

V8.5 materially expands evaluation scope but preserves the core V8 ontology and separation of powers:

- PIT/temporal causality remains.

- Opportunity, Candidate, ExpertWitness and ExecutionCampaign remain distinct.

- Exactly three top-level Oracles remain.

- D-136 and D-141 remain canonical subsystems.

- Kaizen remains the sole normative research/promotion authority.

- Outcome data still cannot mutate the active policy in place.

A V9 boundary should be reopened if implementation requires replacing those primitives, changing the Oracle taxonomy, or converting
the research/evaluation architecture into a fundamentally different autonomous capital-bearing operating system with different authority semantics.
V8.5 is therefore **architecture completion + major evaluation expansion**, not an ontology rewrite.
