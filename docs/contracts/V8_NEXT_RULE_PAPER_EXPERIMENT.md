# Preregistered rule paper experiment

Development scope under the owner's full economic port objective. This is local
native simulation only and grants no live, capital or economic-claim authority.

The default paper path continues to require verified calibrated utility. A separate
explicit experiment window permits prospective measurement of a frozen observer,
grammar and protected campaign policy without pretending a forecast exists. Policy
must be physically frozen before the window starts; backdated registration rejects.
Only canonical opportunities supported by the actual configured observers can be
selected. There are no generated signals, forced trades, fabricated utility values
or calibration receipts. Selection is limited to [start,end); campaign management
continues afterward. Missing data, protection or reconciled risk still rejects.

Both paths share economic identity/causality checks, sizing, native execution and
fee/accounting machinery. Experiment decisions are explicitly RULE_PAPER_EXPERIMENT
and NO_ECONOMIC_CLAIM. Experimental samples do not automatically become calibrated
utility or certified edge. Their selection, funding, statistical methodology and
holdout status require subsequent evaluation. Existing position/funding guards are
not relaxed by experiment mode. A no-trade experiment is a valid observed absence,
not permission to weaken its frozen rule.

Acceptance requires ordinary paper to keep rejecting missing calibration, a frozen
experiment to select only naturally supported opportunities within its window,
native test coverage for resulting orders/costs, and real public capture/replay
without synthetic signals. Positive real-data execution may await a genuine signal;
report absence rather than manufacture it. Full port completion remains separate.
