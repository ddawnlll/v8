| O-021 | Should the D-044 within-family Reality-Check test be pooled across families into one N-configuration statistic, replacing the top-level Bonferroni too? | Build a bar-level (not episode-level) panel: for every bar in the frozen OOS window, each evaluated configuration across all families contributes its episode `net_R` if one is anchored/live at that bar, else 0; run `reality_check_p_value`-style resampling over that panel instead of per-family episode series | Opened by D-044. Within a family, all variants fire on the same setup predicate, so episode grids are already aligned and `src/v8/statistics.reality_check_p_value` applies directly. Across families the grids are disjoint (different `episode_key` sets, different bar counts), so the same function cannot be called on the union without either misaligning series or fabricating a shared index. White's (2000) original construction assumes a common time index for all N rules, which a bar-level zero-filled panel would restore — but that changes what "no signal" means for a family that fires rarely (mostly zeros) versus one that fires often, and needs its own validity argument before being trusted. Admit pooling only after that argument is made and tested on synthetic data with a known ground truth; until then the two-level structure (Bonferroni across families, Reality-Check within a family) stays the interim design and is not merely conservative — it is the version actually verified. |

Per-family `execution_share = n_executed / (n_executed + n_portfolio_rejected)` (the D-027 definition): only 6 of 21 families clear the 0.25 floor (bollinger_breakout 0.273, bollinger_reversion 0.567, trend_pullback 0.313, liquidity_sweep 0.308, fib_projection 0.350, divergence 0.400); 15 sit below, five of them under 0.10 (pandf 0.004, market_profile 0.065, trend_pullback_depth 0.098, obv_adl 0.104, macd 0.116). On the current slate the D-027 attribution-validity gate would score only ~6 families on the OOS and mark the rest `ATTRIBUTION_UNSAFE_*`. Flood ranking (candidates x blocks-caused): bollinger_breakout 14.97 (but 0.273 share — a legitimate family), volume_climax 11.05, market_profile 6.16, floor_trader_pivot 2.71. **De-crowding response (2026-08-07):** the disciplined lever is GATE SELECTIVITY, not retirement — retiring a family on its dev-window execution_share is selection bias (the dev window decides nothing). volume_climax gained the D-055 strict-climax challenger (3-sigma) so the flood tightens only if it survives the frozen-OOS Reality-Check; the other flooders get the same treatment if a declared selectivity challenger exists (market_profile's variant-d distance gate is the natural lever), otherwise their low share stays visible as `ATTRIBUTION_UNSAFE` on the OOS rather than being hidden by deletion. Two readings remain: (a) the coupling is an honest feature of a single-portfolio evaluation and the per-family verdicts are conditional on the OTHER families being present — document it as such and keep the per-family tests; (b) the coupling corrupts the per-family multiplicity accounting (each family's search was implicitly conditioned on the portfolio's exposure history), so a family-level verdict needs either a de-coupled evaluation (one family per run, at the cost of portfolio realism) or a bar-level panel that models the exposure contention (see O-021). Until a preregistered comparison exists, record the blocking matrix alongside every dev/OOS report and do not read a family's verdict as if it were measured in isolation. |
