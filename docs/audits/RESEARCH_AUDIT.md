## Scope and method

The provisional brief was read as a source of terminology, not authority. Research used a source funnel (discovery, triage, targeted extraction, atomic claim mapping). Tavily CLI was available but its account quota was exhausted; its absence is recorded rather than concealed. Built-in web research was used to verify primary/authoritative sources, and the final source set is documented in `SOURCE_MAP.md` and `READING_LIST.md`.

## Audit findings

* Load-bearing external claims have source links in the Source Map; V8-specific architecture advantages are labeled OPEN or PROVISIONAL rather than cited by analogy.
* Local V7 claims are separately bound to `PROJECT_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md`. That audit found the economic authority `FAIL/BLOCKED`; this blocks profitability, promoted-system, and validated-execution language.
* No source is presented as proof that discretionary traders, crypto ontologies, a router, a scorer, or candidate episodes yield durable edge.
* The monograph contains explicit rejection conditions and an experiment registry with no completed experiments.
