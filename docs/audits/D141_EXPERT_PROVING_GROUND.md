## 1. Decision and boundary

D-141 creates the qualification substrate for an `ExpertWitness` version. It answers separately whether a witness implements its declared semantics, obeys point-in-time and feature boundaries, behaves lawfully under declared nuisance transformations, and can be falsified by known defects. It does **not** establish profitability, create an `OpportunityEpisode`, select capital, or promote a witness.

Synthetic, generated, metamorphic, mutation, sabotage, EAST, and historical-allegory outputs are `SEMANTIC_QUALIFICATION` evidence only and must render `NO_ECONOMIC_CLAIM`. Real-tape forced-abstention attribution is diagnostic until a separately authorized frozen chronological OOS gate supplies the required economic authority.

## 2. Four-plane contract

| Plane | Permitted output | Forbidden power |

## 3. Load-bearing invariants

1. **Opportunity sovereignty:** an Expert may observe an existing `OpportunityEpisode` but never creates, deletes, or rekeys one (D-129).
2. **Temporal non-interference:** equal prefixes through decision time have byte-identical decisions and receipts through that time (D-139).
3. **Oracle independence:** a scenario oracle may not import, delegate to, or line-for-line mirror the Expert implementation.
4. **Evidence determinism:** the tuple `(base-world hash, manifest hash, hypothesis, algorithm/version, seed manifest, Expert/code/reference versions, authority configuration)` produces a byte-identical evidence object apart from excluded wall-clock metadata.
5. **No masked hard failure:** critical failure classes—future read, direction inversion, and hidden state—require complete kill coverage; aggregate pass rates never waive them.
6. **No fabricated evidence:** unavailable calibration, outcome, counterfactual, or risk data remains `None`, `NotApplicable`, `Unresolved`, or `NO_ECONOMIC_CLAIM`.
7. **No scalar certification:** a Passport is a conjunction of invariant results and a metric vector, never an auto-promotion score.

## 4. Canonical objects

`BehaviorCard` declares identity, non-economic mechanism hypothesis, habitat, setup/trigger, abstention semantics, symmetry, invalidation/expiry, declared features, nuisance and boundary dimensions, and forbidden dependencies.

`ExpertQualificationManifest` binds that card to an Expert version, scenario families, oracle version, seeds, statistical gate declarations, and maximum authority. `Scenario` is a finite hashable PIT world. `ScenarioOracle` independently maps a scenario to an expected stance or relation. `QualificationRun` binds execution to its manifest and code hashes. `EvidenceObject` carries only its declared authority. `ExpertPassport` aggregates results into a typed `EpistemicVerdict`.

## 6. Scenario and adequacy requirements

The Foundry owns canonical positive, negative, boundary, metamorphic, null, conditional, adversarial, and historical diagnostic worlds. Behavior-space coverage is calculated only over declared cells:

`Coverage(E) = weighted visited required cells / weighted declared required cells`.

Every relation has a precondition. Mandatory where declared: price-scale, LONG/SHORT mirror, irrelevant-feature firewall, prefix non-interference, clone invariance, and execution-order permutation. Invalid inputs or unavailable declared features must fail closed to the existing abstention/no-habitat semantics.

Mutation score is `killed non-equivalent mutants / generated non-equivalent mutants`; equivalent mutants are explicitly excluded. EAST minimizes valid, plausible counterexamples and emits a shrinkable receipt; it must never treat invalid worlds as meaningful failures.

## 7. Statistics and economic firewall

Paired scenario comparisons, confidence sequences, e-processes/e-values, multiplicity control, equivalence/non-inferiority, and risk certificates are allowed only where their assumptions, stopping rule, algorithm version, and scope are manifest-bound. Missing assumptions downgrade the result rather than receiving a numeric placeholder. Their maximum authority is statistical/qualification authority and they never imply an economic result.

Forced-abstention attribution computes a registered baseline-versus-intervention difference while preserving opportunity identity. It emits own contribution, fee drag, displacement, unique capture, and pairwise interaction diagnostics, each explicitly labelled non-promotional unless a separately approved real-tape and frozen-OOS path authorizes more.

## 9. OPEN_PIN triggers

- A requested statistic lacks a pinned estimator, assumptions, or reference vector.
- An oracle cannot be independent of the evaluated implementation.
- A new behavior family has no authoritatively declared Behavior Card.
- A proposed relation is not valid under the Expert's declared feature/venue semantics.
- Real-tape data, intervention semantics, or frozen-OOS authority is unavailable.
- Any D-129, D-136, or D-139 conflict is detected.

Each trigger is `BLOCKED / OPEN_PIN`; no alternate architecture, synthetic economic metric, or silent default is permitted.
