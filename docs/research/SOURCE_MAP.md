# V8 Source Map — evidence-to-decision triage

Prepared against `v8-0.2.html` (the provisional brief).  This is a research map, **not** an architecture decision document.  A source supporting a mechanism does not by itself validate a V8 expert, a feature, or a live-trading rule.

## Source-selection standard

Priority was given to original papers, official research bodies, and maintained technical documentation.  The two practitioner-oriented items below are retained only for engineering/validation context, not as proof of alpha.  Sources were checked through direct page/PDF extraction on 2026-07-31.

## Immediate evidence consequences for the monograph

1. Do not state that “edge is local” as a fact.  It is a testable working hypothesis.  Existing evidence justifies conditioning on mechanisms such as liquidity, order flow, and volatility; it does not establish V8’s proposed habitats.
2. Separate three layers consistently: economic mechanism → observable signature → formal candidate rule.  S1–S4 support the first two in limited contexts; they never uniquely identify the third.
3. `Candidate` is defensible as an auditable data object, but its lifecycle labels must be defined before inspection of outcomes and generated prospectively/point-in-time.
4. Scores advertised as `P(net R > 0)` and `p_trigger` require held-out reliability diagrams, proper scoring rules, and cost-aware decision curves; raw classifier confidence is insufficient (S5).
5. Canonical execution is a research-control decision.  Use it to attribute an expert’s result, then separately test whether adaptive execution adds incremental net utility (S8).
6. The brief’s Phase 2 deterministic baseline is the correct minimum.  Do not add a learned router, shared scorer, ranker, or adaptive manager until its own ablation beats that baseline under S9-style controls.

## Source gaps deliberately left open

- Durable discretionary-trader edge and a validated “trader decision grammar.” Existing general decision theory does not establish either.
- A primary empirical study tying perpetual-futures liquidation fields to profitable, executable V8-style episodes on the proposed venues/horizons.
- A published result proving candidate episodes with non-triggered observations improve a trade-quality model versus trade-only data.
- A published result establishing a stable, universal behavior ontology for crypto.

These are research questions, not citations to manufacture.  The final document should label them `OPEN_QUESTION` or `PROVISIONAL_DECISION` and specify ablations.
