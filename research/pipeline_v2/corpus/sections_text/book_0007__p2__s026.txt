ATTACK THE CURRENCY TREND
Trends are directional, and they’re fast. Trends have a tendency to trade
very technically off simple borderline levels. The 100 and 200 bar MA, trend
lines, remembered lines, and Fibonacci retracements are all the tools you
need. Focus on the five-minute, hourly, and daily charts. Find those levels of significance—those borderlines where the bias is bullish on one side
and bearish on the other. Those borderlines become the basis of low-risk
trades. They define the risk. With risk defined, fear is dispersed. The borderlines also become targets, or exits along the trend highway. Not surprisingly, they also are your exit points for your trades. The more obvious the
level, the better a borderline it is.
Remember that nontrends transition to trends. Train yourself to look
for nontrends. Then look to catch the trend early. You will not know if it
will work until it gets going, but be patient. Look to invest in your business
when it is time to risk profit for greater profit. If the market does not do
what it is supposed to do, get out. If you do, you should find that even
