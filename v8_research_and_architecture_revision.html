Executive Summary

The strongest revision is not “add more Experts.” It is to strengthen the boundary between evidence, interpretation, experiment and runtime execution.

F-ARCH-001

The evidence ledger must preserve generic and corroborating records separately.

Source result: P4 v2.3 reports 1,819 corroborations, 3,809 generic records, 128 dropped records and 115 recorded problems.

Our inference: Aggregation into one strategy label would hide epistemic disagreement.

V8 decision: Keep immutable evidence separate from ontology and runtime mapping.

Sources: P4 full-run registry v2.3

F-ARCH-002

Backtest claims require multiple-testing and transaction-cost controls.

Source result: Harvey and Liu discuss inflated Sharpe statistics after repeated search; execution-cost research measures heterogeneous real-world impact.

Our inference: A backtest is an experiment artifact, not evidence by itself.

V8 decision: Bind search count, cost model and OOS boundary before promotion.

Sources: Evaluating Trading Strategies — Harvey & Liu, Backtesting — Harvey & Liu, Trading Costs — Frazzini, Israel & Moskowitz

F-ARCH-003

Chart/VLM output is not exact market data or self-verifying evidence.

Source result: Chart reasoning evaluations report hallucination, factual error and sensitivity to absent or contradictory chart information.

Our inference: Visual fact, author interpretation and model inference must be separate.

V8 decision: Reject invented prices, timeframes and OHLC values; preserve unreadable as unreadable.

Sources: Chart comprehension limits in LVLMs, ChartHal chart hallucination benchmark

F-ARCH-004

The current V8 contracts are strong on PIT state and deterministic runtime boundaries, but research-to-runtime mapping must remain versioned.

Source result: Current architecture and decision documents separate MarketState, Experts, lifecycle, RiskGate, simulator and Lab.

Our inference: Research findings must not mutate runtime definitions.

V8 decision: Use versioned research annotations and proposal artifacts.

Sources: V8 contracts and decision register

Research Question & Method

Which human trading concepts, market-state dependencies, risk mechanisms and position-management behaviors are missing or under-specified in V8, and which changes can be justified without confusing source claims with runtime truth?

MAP→MARK→REREAD→VERIFY→SYNTHESIZE→MAP TO V8

  • No corpus-wide frontier VLM pass.
  • No strategy is validated from a book example or chart.
  • Testability is downstream, not a discovery filter.

Reading Architecture

Text

Local parse → stable IDs → heading/cross-reference index → BM25/rarity/outlier candidates → cheap mark → bounded reread → exact span alignment → independent verification → immutable finding

Multimodal

Preserve page/image/vector region → local type/hash → selected caption/context skim → high-value crop only → VISIBLE_FACT / AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION / MODEL_INFERENCE → certainty label
